# Ankle X-ray for injury: sources and claims

Date: 2026-11-01 · Branch: `claude/xray-studies`

The author asked for adult injury X-ray studies, one per joint (2026-11-01); the shared shape and the
ankle's minimum content are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-adult-injury-xray-studies-design.md`.
The author chose Weber A, B or C, named in the report by the level of the fibular fracture against the
syndesmosis, with no separate calculator.

## Status: written with the paper databases reachable

Each reference was matched on PubMed, its DOI checked on Crossref, and each claim went in only after
its wording was read in the abstract or an open full text:

- Read in full (Europe PMC full-text API or PubMed Central): Bachmann 2003 (PMC149439, with its
  figure 1), de-las-Heras Romero 2017 (PMC5644422), Ziegler 2023 (PMC9969005), Rydberg 2025
  (PMC12529812), Fonseca 2018 (PMC5771788), York 2020 (PMC7021739), Pflüger 2021 (PMC8419795), Wong
  2015 (PMC4512960), Walther 2023 (PMC10601568) and Chloros 2022 (PMC8788151).
- The ACR ratings (variants and the 1 to 9 scores) were read in the ACR's published ratings table on
  acsearch.acr.org ("Ratings & Appendix", topic 94, Acute Trauma to the Ankle). The JACR narrative was
  not read; only its abstract.
- Every other claim is in its paper's abstract on PubMed.

All 25 references have a DOI. Stiell 1993's DOI on PubMed (10.1001/jama.269.9.1127) does not resolve
on Crossref; Crossref's record for the paper is 10.1001/jama.1993.03500090063034, which the page uses.
Wong 2015 is article 25 on Crossref (PubMed gives "76").

The fifth metatarsal zones reuse the fifth metatarsal page (`src/pages/FifthMetatarsalPage.tsx`): the
same three zones in the same words, the same Lawrence and Botte citation, and a link to the page. No
logic file exists for it. The zone wording was read in Chloros 2022 (Lawrence and Botte's abstract
names the three fracture types but not the zone boundaries).

## Where the sources disagree

- **The Ottawa rule's tenderness sites** (AX1-3, AX1-5). Stiell's rules, as Bachmann draws them,
  name the posterior edge or tip of each malleolus (and the base of the fifth metatarsal and the
  navicular for the foot). The ACR's variant text adds the talus and the calcaneus. The form uses
  Stiell's sites; the Learn tab notes the ACR wording.
- **How far to rotate for the mortise view** (AX2-2). Pneumaticos measured 13.6° (12 to 17°) for a
  true mortise in cadavers; Ziegler gives 20°. Both are given; no chip depends on it.
- **The medial clear space threshold** (AX3-3, AX3-4, AX3-5). Beumer compares it with the superior
  clear space; Park found 5 mm on a dorsiflexion-external rotation stress view; Rydberg says the
  common 4 mm cut-off on non-weight-bearing views has no scientific basis and uses Gregersen's
  weight-bearing criteria (under 5.0 mm, within 1.0 mm of the other side). The form asks which view
  the measurement came from and applies each source's threshold to its own view only.
- **The effusion threshold** (AX5-1, AX5-2). Clark 1995: 13 mm (PPV 82%); Clark 1996: 15 mm (PPV
  83%). The chip states both; the "no fracture seen, consider CT" line starts at 13 mm.
- **Gregersen's side-to-side difference** (AX3-5). Rydberg quotes ≤ 1.0 mm for Gregersen and "less
  than 1 mm" in the flowchart text; the chip uses ≤ 1.0 mm.

## Left out for want of a source that states it

- A sourced share of Weber C injuries with a torn syndesmosis: the only review that gave a figure
  ("up to 80%") is in a journal this site does not use, so the page says only that Weber does not
  consistently predict the syndesmotic injury (Fonseca).
- That the lateral ankle view should include the base of the fifth metatarsal: no source read states
  it. The form asks whether the base is in view, and flags it only when the base is tender (the
  Ottawa foot rule).
- Mortise adequacy criteria beyond the rotation angle, talar tilt in degrees, and the talocrural angle.
- Lauge-Hansen stages, the Herscovici medial malleolar types and the Haraguchi, Bartoníček and Mason
  posterior malleolar types: named by Pflüger, but not needed on a radiograph form; CT is suggested
  instead.
- Pilon (tibial plafond) fractures, the Tillaux-Chaput and Wagstaffe avulsions, and anterior process
  of the calcaneus fractures beyond a free-text "other fractures" field.
- Children: the Ottawa rules were validated in children too, but this study is for adults; the
  ACR's variants apply from 5 years.

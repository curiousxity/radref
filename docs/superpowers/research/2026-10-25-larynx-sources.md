# Larynx CT and MRI: sources and claims

Date: 2026-10-25 · Branch: `claude/sleepy-dijkstra-4w0dpd`

The author chose (2026-10-25): one study for the larynx covering CT for a known cancer, MRI for
a known cancer and CT for hoarseness, with no calculator.

## Status: written with the paper databases reachable

Unlike the October drafts, this study was written with PubMed E-utilities, Crossref and Europe
PMC reachable. Each reference was matched on PubMed, its DOI resolved on Crossref, and each claim
went in only after its wording was read in the abstract or, for Connor 2007 and Joshi 2012, the
open full text (PMC1931513, PMC3624744). Twelve of the references are the ones the 3D larynx
viewer already cited; Connor, Joshi and the three hoarseness papers were added.

One claim is **part**: the AJCC tables come from Connor 2007, which quotes the sixth edition.
Lydiatt's abstract shows the eighth edition changed the T categories of the oral cavity, skin
and nasopharynx, not the larynx, but the sixth-to-seventh wording of the cartilage criterion for
T4a ("through the outer cortex") is not in any open text found. The source note says so.

### Step 9: dislocation, olecranon and variants

Elbow dislocation and olecranon fracture are descriptive fields with no cited rule; the
link between dislocation and an entrapped medial epicondyle is PE7-1. The supracondylar
process is recorded present or absent and gets its own impression line (the author's
decision for variants); no claim is made about it.

### Not included, by choice

- Pulled elbow (radial head subluxation): not included, because no reference above was
  recalled with confidence for its radiographic statement.
- Modified Wilkins IIA/IIB subdivision of Gartland II: not included, for the same reason.
- Sex-specific ossification ages: E2 reports them, but the exact numbers were not recalled
  with confidence, so the age rule uses E1's single approximate ages. The abstract gives the
  girls' 50% ages (1, 5, 5, 9, 9 and 10 years, in E2's order) and a 2-year delay in boys for
  all but the capitellum; see PE2-1 and PE2-2.

## Changes made (2026-10-23, by the author's decision)

The author chose (2026-10-23): add the DOIs now and switch the citations to `<Cite doi>` links,
keep the claims no abstract states, with the "Unchecked draft" note, until they are checked;
rewrite every claim marked "for the author" to its paper's wording (changing a rule where the
paper's boundary differs); and apply every citation correction. Done in the code the same day:
every reference but Gartland and Judet (no DOI anywhere) has its `doi`, the source note says
the references are matched and names what is still unchecked, and
`PediatricElbowStudy.test.ts` pins a DOI on every reference but those two.

- E8 (Weiss): DOI corrected to `10.1097/BPO.0b013e3181b2842c`. E12 (Judet): title changed to
  PubMed's "[Fracture of the radial head in the child]. ... French."
- PE2-1: was the CRITOE order (capitellum, radial head, medial epicondyle, trochlea,
  olecranon, lateral epicondyle) "at roughly 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 years", cited to Iyer. Now
  Cheng's order (olecranon before trochlea) and ages, cited to Cheng: half of girls show the
  centres by 1, 5, 5, 9, 9 and 10 years; boys about 2 years later for all but the capitellum.
  Rule: a centre is expected from the girls' age for a girl and from the boys' age (girls' + 2,
  capitellum 1) for a boy or when the sex is not entered; a new optional "Sex" field in step 1
  sets it. Using the later ages when the sex is not stated is this site's choice. The warning
  now reads "half of girls/boys show it by about age N". The step title drops "(CRITOE)"; the
  lesson notes that the mnemonic swaps olecranon and trochlea. PE2-3's premise (medial
  epicondyle before trochlea) is now cited to Cheng too; its rule is unchanged.
- PE2-2: the girls-earlier statement now carries Cheng's numbers (2-year delay in boys).
- PE4-1: was "normally passes through the middle third ... when the capitellum lies behind it
  ... look for an extension supracondylar fracture" (Rogers). Now: the line is "a valuable aid
  in the search for supracondylar fractures" and when abnormal "other signs of fracture should
  be carefully sought" (Rogers); "passes through the middle third of the capitellum in the
  majority of normal children" is cited to Herman, who states it; Rogers' "younger than 2 1/2
  years" for the anterior third is added beside Herman's under 4. "Extension" is dropped. The
  rule (anterior third can be normal under 4, from Herman, PE4-2) is unchanged.
- PE7-2: was "radiographs ... underestimate the true displacement seen on CT". Now: standard
  radiographs are not sufficient to measure anterior displacement nor accurate enough to
  measure medial displacement; the lateral view underestimated anterior displacement (0.9
  against 8.8 mm) and the AP view overestimated medial displacement (3.5 against 0.3 mm); an
  internal oblique view came closer. No rule changed (there was no threshold).

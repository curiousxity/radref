# Neonatal chest X-ray: sources and claims

Date: 2026-11-01 · Branch: `claude/xray-studies`

The author asked for a study page for "baby x-rays" and chose the neonatal (NICU) chest radiograph
(2026-11-01). Why, and what was left out of scope, is in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-neonatal-chest-xray-study-design.md`.

## Status: written with the paper databases reachable

Written the same way as the brachial plexus study: each reference was matched on PubMed, its DOI
checked on Crossref, and each claim went in only after its wording was read in the abstract or an
open full text:

- Read in full through the Europe PMC full-text API: Lovrenski 2025 (PMC12226615, CC BY), Gallacher
  2016 (PMC4818233, CC BY-NC), Dahm 2025 (PMC12281396, CC BY), Cerone 2022 (PMC8870647, CC BY), Rocha
  2018 (PMC6110042, CC BY), D'Andrea 2022 (PMC8841780, CC BY), Verheij 2010 (PMC2866966, CC BY), Naseh
  2026 (PMC13221281, CC BY), Zubi 2023 (PMC10497369, CC BY) and Hauri-Hohl 2008 (PMC2254655, CC BY-NC).
- Every other claim is in its paper's abstract on PubMed (Blayney's PMC record holds only the
  abstract).

All 24 references have a DOI. No claim was kept that a source does not state; the claims left out
for that reason are listed at the end.

**Tip ranges.** The form records each tip as the vertebral body it overlies, and the chip compares it
with the range quoted below. Where sources give different ranges, the chip uses the one named in
"Where the sources disagree" and the chip text names the other.

## Where the sources disagree

- **UVC tip by vertebral level** (N3-7, N3-9, N3-13, N3-4). D'Andrea's vertebral-body method and Zubi
  give T8 to T9; Verheij gives T9 to T10 (T8 adequate in some); Narla (1991) puts the tip in the right
  atrium, where the newer reviews put it at the IVC–right atrium junction. The chip uses T8 to T9,
  the range two of the three vertebral sources give and the most widely cited of them (D'Andrea), and
  names Verheij's range on a tip at T8 or T10. Hoellering found the heart-shadow method more accurate
  than any vertebral level (N3-8), so the form asks for both, and a chip and a warning fire when they
  disagree.
- **UAC tip** (N3-4, N3-9, N3-11, N3-13). High: T6 to T10 (Narla, Verheij), T6 to T9 (Naseh), T7 to
  T9 (Zubi). Low: L3 to L5 (Narla, Verheij, Zubi), L3 to L4 (Naseh). The chip uses Narla's T6 to T10
  and L3 to L5 (the radiology source, and the widest-cited), and names Naseh's narrower end on a tip
  at T10 or L5.
- **ETT tip** (N3-1, N3-2, N3-3, N3-4). Blayney: the body of T1. Thayyil: T1 or T2. Cerone: top of
  T1 to bottom of T2 acceptable. Narla: 1.5 cm above the carina. The chip uses T1 to T2, which holds
  Blayney's T1 target, and names that target on a tip at T2. No chip uses the carina distance, since
  the form records levels.
- **Inspiration** (N2-1, N2-2). The ESPR's general paediatric figure is at least eight posterior ribs;
  NICU guidelines, as Dahm quotes them, use 7 to 9. The chip uses 7 to 9 and adds Dahm's finding that
  the rib count is a weak guide to volume.
- **BPD definition** (N4-7). ESPR: born before 32 weeks and on oxygen at 36 weeks' postmenstrual age.
  Gallacher: oxygen for at least 28 days and at 36 weeks' corrected age. Both are given; the chip
  quotes the ESPR's.

## Left out for want of a source that states it

- PICC tip ranges from a second source: only Zubi (citing a nursing guideline) gives T3 to T5 and
  T8 to T10; Nowlen's "outside the heart shadow" is added beside it.
- A vertebral range for the gastric tube or the chest drain tip, and a size grading for pneumothorax.
- Radiograph signs of a tension pneumothorax (mediastinal shift, flattened diaphragm): no neonatal
  source read states them, so mediastinal shift is recorded in step 6 with no tension rule.
- The UVC crossing the foramen ovale to the left atrium, the UAC's downward loop through the
  internal iliac artery, oesophageal intubation on the film, and the tracheostomy tube.
- Normal heart size (a cardiothoracic ratio for neonates), the normal thymus and its sail shape,
  thymic shrinkage with stress, and bone findings (rib count, fractures, vertebral anomalies): the
  bones field records them with no rule.
- Asymmetric clearing after surfactant, pulmonary haemorrhage on the film, Northway's BPD stages, and
  ETT depth formulas (the 7-8-9 rule).
- The abdomen beyond what shows on a chest film (no NEC), and congenital lung malformations other
  than CDH and oesophageal atresia.

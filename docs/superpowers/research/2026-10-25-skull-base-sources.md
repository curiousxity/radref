# Skull base MRI and CT: sources and claims

Date: 2026-10-25 · Branch: `claude/sleepy-dijkstra-4w0dpd`

The author chose (2026-10-25): one study for the skull base covering MRI for a cranial nerve
palsy, MRI for perineural spread and CT for a skull base mass, with no calculator.

## Status: written with the paper databases reachable

Written the same way as the larynx study: each reference was matched on PubMed, its DOI checked
on Crossref, and each claim went in only after its wording was read in the abstract or, for
Tamhankar 2013, the open full text (PMC3795864, read through the PubMed E-utilities). Unpaywall
found no free copy of the paywalled papers except Nader 2019 (its abstract was enough).

Nemzek 1998, Russo 1997, Eldevik 2000 and Yeom 2013 are free on PubMed Central, but as scanned
PDFs that the PMC site would only serve behind a robot check, so they were read in their
abstracts. Every claim below is in an abstract.

Russo 1997, Nemzek 1998 and Eldevik 2000 have no DOI on PubMed or Crossref, so they are cited in
plain text and the study test exempts them by name.

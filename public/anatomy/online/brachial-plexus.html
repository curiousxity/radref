          About this model

          Bones, muscles and the subclavian artery and vein are BodyParts3D meshes, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Meshes were simplified for this file.

          Nerves, cord, thecal sac, axillary and brachial vessels, the cervical rib and every pathology are drawn onto those meshes. Anchor points were measured from the mesh: the five right neural foramina, the gap between scalenus anterior and medius, the course of the subclavian artery over the first rib, the space deep to pectoralis minor, the coracoid tip, the suprascapular notch, the humeral head centre and the humeral shaft.

          In this cadaver the space under the clavicle is very tight (about mm where the bundle crosses), and BodyParts3D's own artery mesh touches subclavius there, so the divisions are packed closely around the artery. Arm and neck movement is a simplified rig for showing direction of force, not measured kinematics.
